# QRious Access

G'day,

We had this new "SECUREPASS" contraption put in at the visitor centre. Supposed to be some fancy access control setup, all modern and clever-like. Visitors have to download an app, which spits out one of those QR code things, and then the kiosk at the gate scans it to let 'em through. That's the theory anyway. In practice, the gate just sits there wide open like a paddock with a busted fence, and the app crashes more often than an old valve radio with dodgy wiring.

The young blokes who built it reckon it's still in "early access" and say they're working on fixes. They call it "state of the art". If you ask me, it's a load of nonsense.

Anyway, I thought I'd have a bit of a poke around myself. Took the side panel off the gate and clipped on a logic analyser to an SD card reader. A developer came to fiddle with the kiosk, and when his app kept crashing he stuck his phone up to the scanner and printed the QR out on paper. I got a recording of it all, but I can't see anything in there that looks like a QR code.

Reckon you could give an old bloke a hand?
